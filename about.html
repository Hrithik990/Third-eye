About Us

About Us

Welcome to Third Eye Healing Centre

At Third Eye Healing Centre, we offer daily yoga, meditation, breathwork, and sound healing sessions in Thamel, Kathmandu.

Our classes help you build physical strength, improve flexibility, and find quiet time away from busy schedules. Beginners and experienced practitioners are always welcome.

Our Founder

How the Centre Started

Third Eye Healing Centre was established to provide a dedicated, welcoming studio for regular yoga practice and sound healing in the heart of Kathmandu.

Pradeep Pandey
Founder

We opened this studio to give people a calm space to step away from daily routines, focus on their breathing, and build sustainable health habits through yoga and sound meditation.

Instructors

Our Teaching Team

Our classes are guided by resident instructors who specialize in sound therapy and traditional yoga.

Bimal Pandey
Sound Healing Instructor

Sound therapy works with the vibrations of singing bowls to calm the mind and ease muscle tension. It provides deep rest for anyone carrying stress.

Chandra Sapkota
